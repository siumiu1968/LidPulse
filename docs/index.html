Which MacBook Pro has the glowing Apple logo?

Apple's 14-inch and 16-inch MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR Display use tandem OLED and have an illuminated logo on the lid. The separate 14-inch M6 model uses Liquid Retina XDR. Check for PROLED XDR in the product name. Apple's PROLED XDR announcement · Apple's illuminated logo guide · Apple's M6 announcement

LidPulse was tested on an M5 Max PROLED XDR MacBook Pro (Mac17,10) running macOS 27.2. It requires a physical lid light and a compatible controller.

Five ways to use the Apple logo light

Steady

Set the brightness of the Apple logo independently of the screen.

Breathe

Fade the logo up and down at a pace you choose.

Battery

Let the logo dim as your battery runs down, within your chosen brightness.

Music

Pulse along with music playing on your Mac. Set the intensity or go dark between beats.

Agents

See when Codex or Claude Code is working, idle, or waiting for your attention.

Install on a supported Mac

Open the DMG, drag LidPulse into Applications, then open it. Find the glowing Apple logo in the menu bar. Use Settings to switch languages, open at login, or connect an agent.

How do I dim or turn off the MacBook Pro logo?

Use the brightness slider or switch Logo Light off in the menu bar. Both controls work without typing a Terminal command.

Can the logo stay lit when I close the lid?

On supported hardware, turn on Keep Logo Lit With Lid Closed to hold your selected brightness with the lid closed. Displays are put to sleep, audio analysis and animations pause, and your previous mode resumes when you open the lid. Your Mac stays awake for up to 30 minutes per session without administrator approval. Battery charge below 20% while on battery power, or serious or critical thermal pressure, ends the session early. Turning this off, disabling the light, or quitting restores normal sleep. Do not put your Mac in a bag while this is enabled.

How do I update LidPulse?

Choose Check for Updates in the menu or Settings. Automatic checks and installation are enabled by default and can be changed in Settings. Updates are signed and verified. Users on 1.0.x need to download this release once to enable in app updates.

This release is ad hoc signed and has not been notarized by Apple. macOS may block a downloaded copy at first launch.

Full brightness, audio and agent permissions

On a Mac with brightness limits, choose Unlock Full Brightness to have macOS request administrator approval. You can restore the previous limits in Settings. The app does not ask for admin approval when it starts.

Music mode requests system audio access. Audio is analyzed locally and is not saved or uploaded. For Codex, review and trust the installed hooks in Codex Settings before using the agent light.
