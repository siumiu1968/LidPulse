# LidPulse 1.1.0

- 新增電量模式，Logo 亮度隨電池電量調整。
- 改善合蓋亮燈，保留所選模式同亮度，毋須管理員授權。
- 合蓋保持清醒最多 10 分鐘；低電量或系統報告升溫時恢復正常睡眠。
- 新增檢查更新、自動下載及安裝，並驗證更新簽章。

合蓋亮燈會令 Mac 保持清醒，請勿放入袋中。最高亮度解鎖仍由 macOS 要求管理員授權。

---

- Added Battery mode: logo brightness follows battery charge.
- Improved closed-lid lighting with your selected mode and brightness, without administrator approval.
- Closed-lid sessions last up to 10 minutes and stop on low battery or elevated thermal pressure.
- Added signed in-app updates with automatic checking, downloading and installation.

Closed-lid lighting keeps your Mac awake. Do not put it in a bag. Full brightness unlocking still requires macOS administrator approval.
