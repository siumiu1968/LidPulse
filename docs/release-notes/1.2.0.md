# LidPulse 1.2.0

- 合蓋亮燈時會要求顯示器熄屏。
- 合蓋期間以設定亮度固定發光，暫停音訊分析同呼吸、音樂及 AI 燈效動畫；開蓋後恢復原本模式。
- 減少合蓋期間嘅定時更新，保留 30 分鐘上限、高溫同低電量保護。

Mac 會保持清醒，其他程式仍然可以運作；實際耗電及發熱會受其他工作影響。

---

- Requests display sleep during closed-lid lighting.
- Holds your selected brightness with audio analysis and animated effects paused. Opening the lid restores your previous mode.
- Reduces periodic work while preserving the 30-minute limit, thermal protection and low-battery cutoff.

Your Mac stays awake and other apps can continue running. Power consumption and heat depend on other workloads.
